# Code of Conduct

SocialMonitor follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, keep feedback about the work, and make
room for people of every background and level of experience. Harassment,
personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated in issues,
discussions, pull requests or anywhere else the project is represented.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to **conduct@shades.md**. Every report
is read by the maintainers at Shades Media Group, handled confidentially, and
answered. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or
permanently ban anyone who breaks this code, following the enforcement
guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
