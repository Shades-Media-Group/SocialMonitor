## [Unreleased]

### Added

- **Reading the networks.** Paced, read-only readers for TikTok, Instagram and Facebook keep every post and comment in a local SQLite database. TikTok is read from its public pages with no account, with each commenter's photo, profile link and any sticker or picture.
- **Connect your own TikTok.** A brand can connect its own TikTok account through TikTok's sign-in, for exact video figures and its follower count. Setup in [docs/tiktok.md](docs/tiktok.md).
- **AI sorting.** Claude sorts every comment by kind (complaint, question, suggestion, praise), urgency and feeling, describes each post, drafts replies and translates. People can correct any rating.
- **The dashboard.** An overview with figures against the period before, a chart with unusual days marked, accounts, the newest comments, what people say, problems named, words, when people comment and the most active people.
- **Comments as conversations.** Each comment as a bubble with the person's photo, replies on a thread line, the brand's answers marked, tags for kind, feeling and answer state. Filters keep threads whole.
- **Reply queue.** Comments the brand already answered on the network close themselves; a reply target per brand marks overdue ones; urgent ones come first.
- **Posts.** A table by default, or pictures shown whole with two to six per row, and "what works" by format and hashtag.
- **Calendar.** Days, weeks and months shaded by activity, with posts and comments per day and the day's posts and comments in a popover.
- **Words to watch.** Products, campaigns, other brands or topics, counted in comments against the period before.
- **Reports.** A graphic PDF report, Excel and CSV exports, a negative comments report grouped by kind and emailed per brand, and a history of every file made.
- **People and sign-in.** Accounts with passwords and an emailed code, super admins, and admins or viewers per brand. Optional remote access through Cloudflare Tunnel and Access.
- **Five languages** in the dashboard (English, Romanian, Russian, French, Chinese) and eleven on the website, with every choice kept per account.
- **An MCP server**, so Claude can read the overview, comments and posts, run updates, rate comments and make reports.
- **The website**, socialmonitor.shades.md, with privacy policy and terms of use.
- The repository: licence, contributing, security, support and privacy guides, CI, CodeQL and issue forms.
- The logo, "Share of voice": a speech bubble cut like a pie chart, generated by `yarn brand`.
